🍽️ Restaurant Sales, Orders & Customer Analysis --- Power BI

An interactive Power BI data analytics dashboard built to analyze
restaurant sales, orders, customers, delivery performance, restaurant
performance, and city-level business trends.

📊 Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to transform restaurant/order data into an
interactive business dashboard. The report is divided into key
analytical areas:

Sales & Order Analysis

Customer Analysis

Restaurant Performance Analysis

City-level Analysis

Delivery & Order Status Analysis

Time-based Trends

The dashboard contains data covering 2023--2025 for major
order/revenue trends, while the customer sign-up analysis also includes
historical sign-up activity beginning in 2022.

📌 Dashboard Sections

1. Sales & Order Analysis

The sales/order dashboard provides an overview of:

Total revenue

Average order value

Total orders

Revenue trend by year

Order trend by year

Order status distribution

Delivery status analysis

Revenue by city

Order volume by city

The order-status analysis shows Delivered, Cancelled, and Delayed
orders, while delivery analysis compares Late vs On-Time deliveries.

2. Customer Analysis

The customer dashboard focuses on customer behavior and segmentation.

Key analysis includes:

Total customers

Customer percentage

Orders per customer

Repeat customers

Customer trend over time

Customer sign-up trend

New vs Repeat customers

Customer type distribution

Customers by city

Customer-level order and revenue analysis

The dashboard reports approximately 10K repeat customers and 10K
new customers, with customer types including New, Returning, and
Premium.

3. Restaurant Analysis

The restaurant dashboard evaluates restaurant performance using:

Total restaurants

Average rating

Average votes

Revenue per restaurant

Restaurant revenue trend

Restaurant order trend

Restaurant rating buckets

Cost buckets

Top ordered restaurants

Restaurant-level order and revenue analysis

Restaurant rating categories shown in the report include Good, Poor,
Average, and Excellent, while cost categories include Premium, Mid
Range, and Budget Friendly.

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard can help answer questions such as:

How is revenue changing over time?

How many orders are being delivered, cancelled, or delayed?

What proportion of deliveries are late vs on time?

Which cities generate the highest revenue and order volume?

How many customers are new vs repeat?

How frequently do customers place orders?

Which restaurants receive the most orders?

How do restaurant ratings relate to restaurant performance?

How does restaurant cost category vary across the business?

Which customer segments contribute to the customer base?
